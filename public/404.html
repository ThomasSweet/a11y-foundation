Even a dead end can practise what this site preaches

Error pages are where accessibility usually gets forgotten. This one ships zero JavaScript. The colours follow your colour scheme through light-dark(), the entrance and the button's shape-morph switch off under your reduced-motion preference, and the morph itself is corner-shape easing from squircle to scoop — in browsers without it, the button is simply a rounded button.

Every link here is a real link, the disclosure you just opened is a native <details>, and all of it works with a keyboard. A dead end should still be perceivable, operable, understandable, and robust.
